<h1>About RadixHomeWork</h1>

<p>
  RadixHomeWork is my personal workshop: a place for projects that begin
  as ideas and slowly grow into something tangible.
</p>

<p>
  The name is a small play on <em>radis</em> / <em>radix</em>, while
  <em>radix</em> also means “root” in Latin. The root is therefore both
  a hidden joke and a fitting metaphor for projects that grow over time.
</p>

<p>
  <em>Personal projects, made from home.</em>
</p>
